PayShield protects the money your household has already spoken for and gives you one honest Safe to Spend balance.

BUILD YOUR PAYCHECK PLAN
Create protected buckets for rent, utilities, insurance, childcare, savings, or anything else that matters. Choose the target, due date, priority, and protection rule for every bucket.

PUT EVERY PAYCHECK TO WORK
Connect the account where income arrives, define how deposits should be recognized, and let your priorities determine what gets funded first. If a paycheck is short, PayShield follows the order you set.

SPEND WITH CLARITY
Safe to Spend excludes protected commitments. Card controls keep everyday purchases inside that amount, while approved billers can use only the bucket assigned to them.

STAY IN CONTROL
Move money, schedule bills, verify payees, lock your card, change protection rules, or release funds with a visible recovery plan. Every action appears in your household activity record.

ONE HOUSEHOLD MEMBERSHIP
PayShield is $19 per month for the household. Purchase and subscription management are handled securely through Google Play.
