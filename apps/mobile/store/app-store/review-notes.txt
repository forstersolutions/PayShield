PayShield requires an authenticated household account. Use the credentials entered in App Review Information.

Suggested review path:
1. Complete sign-in and identity onboarding.
2. Open Plan and edit a protected bucket.
3. Open Account and connect the designated sandbox institution through Plaid Link.
4. Review Safe to Spend, Bills, Activity, card lock, export, and subscription restore.

No reviewer should enter personal financial credentials. The review account and connected institution must use the configured provider sandbox. Contact support@graystontechnologies.com for immediate review assistance.
