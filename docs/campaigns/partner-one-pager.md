# Partner One-Pager

Status: ready for counsel review, not approved for launch.
Channel: partner.

PayShield is a paycheck-control app for households, employers, and partners that
want a clearer Safe to Spend experience.

The current product surface delivers paycheck buckets, protected-bucket
messaging, shortfall awareness, and reserve recovery controls without bank
credentials or money movement.

Account opening, card controls, and money movement stay locked until approved
provider credentials, disclosures, and operating controls are active. The current
public app does not provide financial services.

Partner discussion goal:

Evaluate household demand, user language, employer interest, and future partner
requirements before any regulated financial-service launch.
